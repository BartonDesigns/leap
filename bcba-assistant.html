Interview & session transcripts

Paste or load transcripts of recorded caregiver interviews, intake calls, or observation sessions. When transcripts are present, Claude treats them as the primary evidence for choosing goals—quoting what caregivers said and what was observed—and uses the assessment scores to confirm and measure them.

Before you add a transcript

  • Claude reads text, not audio or video. Use the on-device transcriber below, or load existing captions/transcripts (Zoom, Teams, Meet, Otter, Rev) as .txt, .vtt, .srt, or .md files.
  • Transcripts only capture speech. For observation videos, add what you saw in Observer notes—gestures, eye contact, play, behavior incidents with timestamps.
  • Remove names first. List every real name below and press Redact names before exporting or sending to Claude.
